# Customer User Guide

Welcome to the Fresh Market customer mobile & web application.

---

## 1. Sign Up & Sign In
* **Register**: Create a new account using your email and password. Your role defaults to *Customer*.
* **Profile Info**: You can add your display name, phone number, and upload a profile picture.

---

## 2. Storefront Browsing & Search
* **Homepage**: View featured products, banners, and current promotional offers.
* **Categories**: Tap categories (e.g. Meat, Poultry, Frozen) to browse catalog items.
* **Search**: Use the storefront search bar to find products instantly.

---

## 3. Cart & Checkout Workflow
1. **Add to Cart**: Tap the add button on any product card.
2. **Review Cart**: Tap the shopping bag icon. You can adjust quantities or input a Coupon code.
3. **Checkout**: 
   * Choose your delivery address.
   * Review total pricing (pricing is dynamically validated against current repository prices).
   * Confirm the checkout. The app reserves stock atomically using FIFO allocation.

---

## 4. Tracking Orders
* Go to **My Orders** in the profile menu.
* Select an order to view items, totals, delivery status (e.g. Pending, Out for Delivery, Delivered), and history.
* Customers can cancel **Pending** orders directly from this page.
